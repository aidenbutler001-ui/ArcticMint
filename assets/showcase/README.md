# Showcase

Models made with ArcticMint on our own PC from a one-sentence prompt each. Nothing was modelled or painted by hand.
How they were made, with the prompts, seeds and settings, is in [`docs/showcase/`](../../docs/showcase/README.md).
(The older examples inherited from upstream are in [`../upstream-examples/`](../upstream-examples/README.md).)
